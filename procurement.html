Read before you proceed

The fee (7%, 10%, or 12%) is calculated on the total cost of your items in naira, based on the current yuan exchange rate at the time of your order. Always confirm the current rate before proceeding.

Once payment for the products has been made and the order placed with the supplier, cancellation is not possible. Please be sure before making product payment.
